# Agency Playbook

## Brand
FitTrack Pro

## Product/Service
Smart fitness tracker with AI coaching. Wearable device + app. $149 one-time purchase + optional $9.99/mo premium coaching plan.

## Target Audience
Health-conscious adults 28-45 who want to get fit but struggle with consistency. They've tried Apple Watch or Fitbit but found them too passive — they want something that tells them what to do, not just tracks what they did.

## Performance Goals
- ROAS target: > 3.0
- CPA target: < $35
- A winning ad: ROAS > 4.0 or CPA < $25 sustained over 7 days

## Extracted Insights

### Ad Philosophy
- "We test angles aggressively — 10 new angles per week, kill fast, scale winners" _(Source: strategy-call-2026-02.md)_
- "UGC outperforms polished studio content for us 4:1" _(Source: strategy-call-2026-02.md)_

### Audience Insights
- Primary buyer is a "lapsed athlete" — someone who was fit in their 20s and wants to get back to it _(Source: customer-interviews.md)_
- "I don't need another thing that tells me I walked 8,000 steps" — common sentiment from prospects _(Source: customer-interviews.md)_

### Creative Preferences
- Preferred formats: UGC selfie-style, talking head, before/after transformation
- Tone: Motivational but not preachy. Real, not aspirational.
- Video lengths that work: 30s for cold traffic, 60s for retargeting

### Performance Rules
- Kill an ad after 3x target CPA with zero conversions
- Scale winners by duplicating campaign, not increasing budget above $100/day
- Test one variable at a time

### Competitive Context
- Main competitors: Apple Watch, Fitbit, Whoop
- Our edge: Active coaching (not passive tracking). The AI tells you exactly what to do today.
